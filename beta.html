Global Louisville Action Plan

Unveiled in December 2016, the Global Louisville Action plan is a collaborative effort between the Mayor’s Office, regional chamber of commerce Greater Louisville Inc., and hundreds of community members. It outlines key strategies to attract, retain, and grow our foreign-born population and workforce by providing an overview of the region’s foreign-born population and a set of action steps to increase the number of immigrants here and their capacity to thrive.

A 12 page booklet with covers was created to showcase the plan, with custom infographics throughout.

The Natural Grip

The Natural Grip became a contestant on the Emmy award winning ABC show Shark Tank and asked us to help them prepare for the airing. We implemented and designed two branded landing pages, new custom illustrations, optimized the site for search engines, set up a new e-commerce ordering system and set up hosting for the influx of show traffic.

  • Between November 7th-10th (episode aired 11/7/14) there were more than 65,000 visits to the Natural Grip website.
  • This four-day total surpassed the total number of visits to the site over the year previous to the episode airing.
  • Year-over-year traffic was up by 5,000%.
  • During the Shark Tank campaign, the average user spent nearly six minutes on the site, an increase of 500% from the same period a year earlier.
  • 20% of users visited the shop page during the Shark Tank campaign.
  • Traffic to the site was largely driven by organic search (viewers searching for Natural Grip in their search bars; 52%), and referral traffic (news coverage of the episode).
  • An article on Yahoo! Finance posted after the episode aired drove 16,000 visits to the site.
