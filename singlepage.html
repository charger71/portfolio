UX Case Study: Redesigning a Local Library Website

An end-to-end user experience redesign focusing on accessibility, content discoverability, and mobile responsiveness.

The local library's website was outdated and hard to navigate, especially on mobile devices. The goal was to modernize the interface and improve content discovery.

Conducted user interviews and usability testing to identify pain points and understand user goals. Accessibility audits were also performed.

Created wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes incorporating feedback from stakeholders and users. Focused on responsive and accessible design.

Iterative usability testing helped refine navigation and interaction flows. Adjustments were made to ensure color contrast and keyboard navigation met standards.

The redesigned website increased mobile traffic by 35%, improved user satisfaction, and reduced bounce rates by 20%. Accessibility compliance improved significantly.

HTML5, CSS3 (Grid & Flexbox), JavaScript, ARIA for accessibility, Figma for design & prototyping, and Lighthouse for audits.
